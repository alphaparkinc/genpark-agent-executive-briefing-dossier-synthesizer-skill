# genpark-agent-executive-briefing-dossier-synthesizer-skill

Cross-departmental executive briefing and morning dossier synthesizer aggregating Slack, email threads, and Jira updates into structured KPI summaries and risk signals.

## Architecture
- **Input**: Raw daily metrics and operations stream.
- **Engine**: KPI trend weighting, risk categorization, and summary synthesis.
- **Output**: 3-minute executive brief with health score and top risks.
